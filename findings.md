\# Findings



Olist Brazilian e-commerce, \~99k orders, Sep 2016 – Sep 2018.

q1–q4 done; q5–q6 pending.



Scope: activity before 2017-01 is negligible (Nov 2016 is empty), so trend

analysis starts at 2017-01 to avoid calendar gaps.



\## q1 — Monthly revenue

Grew from \~R$137k (Jan 2017) to a \~R$1.1M/month plateau by mid-2018. Roughly

doubled over 2017, flattened in 2018. The 3-month average confirms real growth,

not spikes.



\## q2 — Cohort retention

Near zero. \~0.48% return the month after their first order, \~1.9% ever return.

Cause: 96.9% of customers order exactly once. This is a buy-once marketplace,

not a recurring business.



\## q3 — Top sellers per category

Category leaders: office\_furniture (\~R$219k), watches\_gifts (\~R$218k),

bed\_bath\_table (\~R$193k). Some categories are run by one dominant seller

(agro\_industry: R$33.8k vs R$8.4k for #2); others are close. Revenue and order

count don't always track — one health\_beauty seller hits similar revenue on

1,048 orders as another on 166.



\## q4 — Late delivery by state

Strongly regional. Worst are North/Northeast, far from the São Paulo hub:

Alagoas 23.9%, Maranhão 19.6%, Piauí 16.0%, Ceará 15.3%. States near SP are in

single digits. Nationally \~8% of delivered orders are late.



\## q5 — RFM segmentation

Scored each customer 1-4 on Recency, Frequency, Monetary (snapshot = last order

\+ 1 day). Frequency is a dead axis: 96.9% order once, so three of the four F

buckets are all single-order customers and the score is just sort position.

Segmentation effectively runs on Recency and Monetary, which have real spread.



\## q6 — Review score vs delivery delay

Lateness sharply lowers ratings. Early/on-time orders average \~4.3 stars; this

drops to 3.46 when 1-5 days late and 1.79 when more than 5 days late. Delivery

delay is a strong, direct driver of bad reviews.
